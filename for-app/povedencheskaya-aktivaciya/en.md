## What this changes for Opora

1. **A separate "one small action" skill is justified.** Activation is a component
   with a measurable contribution inside digital programmes (−1.83 points on the
   depression scale), and the HAP effect ran through activation rather than mood.
   The honest wording: "not to lift your mood, but to get you moving".
2. **No norm of "N actions", no daily diary.** There are no dose data. Count the
   attempt, not the result; do not show overdue items or streaks — guilt increases
   avoidance, the very thing activation works on.
3. **"By values" is a BA technique, not a proven method.** No comparative RCT of
   "by values versus by pleasure" was found.

## What changes in Opora

- **Skill:** one concrete action for today, counting attempts instead of results;
  reference to
  [`furukawa-2021-icbt-components`](../sources/furukawa-2021-icbt-components.md, `meta`).
- **No streaks, overdue items or points** for completed actions: the dose is not
  established [`jia-2025-digital-ba`](../sources/jia-2025-digital-ba.md, `meta`).
- **Prognosis:** without "it will cure you"; the effect of digital programmes fades
  by 12 months [`jia-2025-digital-ba`](../sources/jia-2025-digital-ba.md, `meta`).

## Not found

- No RCT varies the size of a step or the number of actions per week — there is
  nothing to base an in-app norm on.
- BA in active suicidality has not been studied: such participants are excluded
  from RCTs, and the app must not present activation as a means against suicide
  risk.
- No separate studies of BA in pronounced apathy or anhedonia.
