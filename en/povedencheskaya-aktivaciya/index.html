Behavioural activation

updated: 2026-11-25

Not a substitute for a doctor. If life is in danger, call 112.

What it is

Behavioural activation (BA) is a brief psychotherapy that changes behaviour and environment rather than thoughts: a person plans concrete actions and regains access to what reinforces them uphoff-2020-behavioural-activation meta. It is not "positive thinking" and not the advice to "just go for a walk": the work targets avoidance and a schedule of actions, not convincing yourself that everything is fine.

What is known

Better than usual care — but the effect is fragile. Cochrane: 53 studies, 5495 participants; BA versus usual care — RR 1.40 (1.10–1.78), 7 RCTs, 1533 people, moderate certainty. In worst-case and intention-to-treat analyses the difference disappears uphoff-2020-behavioural-activation meta. "It works" is confirmed; "it works for everyone" is not.

No worse than CBT. BA versus CBT — RR 0.99 (0.92–1.07), 5 RCTs, 601 people uphoff-2020-behavioural-activation meta. In COBRA, 440 people over 12 months: PHQ-9 8.4 versus 8.4, a difference of 0.1 points (−1.3…1.5) against a non-inferiority margin of 1.9 richards-2016-cobra rct.

No worse than antidepressants. One RCT of 141 people: RR 1.77 (1.14–2.76) uphoff-2020-behavioural-activation meta. In the Ekers meta-analysis — SMD −0.42 (−0.83…0.00), 4 RCTs, 283 people: the upper bound reaches zero, so "BA beats pills" is not a conclusion of that work ekers-2014-ba-update meta.

A large but heterogeneous effect against control. SMD −0.74 (−0.91…−0.56), 25 RCTs, 1088 people; quality was low in most studies and follow-up short ekers-2014-ba-update meta. Individual BA: g 0.85 (0.57–1.1), 22 RCTs, 819 people; low risk of bias only — g 0.56 (0.09–1.03), prediction interval −0.85…1.98, that is, in some populations there may be no effect cuijpers-2023-individual-ba meta.

In severe depression, no weaker than pills. An RCT of 241 people: among the more severely depressed BA was comparable to antidepressant medication, and both significantly outperformed CBT dimidjian-2006-ba-ct-medication rct. This refutes "BA is only for mild cases".

In an app it works, but not for long. 81 people over 8 weeks: no significant difference between BA and a mindfulness programme; BA was better for the more severe and mindfulness for the less severe ly-2014-app-ba-mindfulness rct. A meta-analysis of digital BA: 17 RCTs, symptoms lower at 2, 3 and 6 months, no difference at 12 months (p=0.82) jia-2025-digital-ba meta. App-based psychotherapies in general: SMD −0.51 (−0.69…−0.33), 12 trials, 2859 people serrano-ripoll-2022-apps meta.

Inside digital programmes BA is a working component. A component network meta-analysis of iCBT: 76 RCTs, 17 521 people; adding BA gave −1.83 points on the depression scale (CrI −2.90…−0.80), while relaxation may be harmful (1.20; CrI 0.17–2.27) furukawa-2021-icbt-components meta.

It transfers to lay counsellors and holds for a year. HAP, India, primary care: 495 people, counsellors without specialist training; at 12 months BDI-II was 4.45 points lower (−7.26…−1.63), remission aPR 1.36 (1.15–1.61), and the effect was mediated by activation itself (β=−2.62; −3.28…−1.97) weobong-2017-hap-12m rct.

What is not proven

  • Dose. How many actions per day or week — not established; the authors of the digital BA meta-analysis state directly that the optimal "dose" is unknown jia-2025-digital-ba meta. 8 sessions in COBRA and an 8-week programme are programme parameters, not a norm.
  • "Small steps". No RCT was found in which the size of a step was a factor. The mechanism "avoidance → less reinforcement → less activity" is theory, not a measurement of step size.
  • Suicidality. In HAP suicidal behaviour: aPR 0.71 (0.51–1.01), p=0.06 — a signal without significance; COBRA excluded acutely suicidal people and those who had attempted in the previous 2 months. BA is not proven as a means against suicide risk; that is not its task.
  • Apathy and anhedonia as a separate group. No separate RCTs were found.
  • Dropout. BA versus usual care for dropout: RR 1.64 (0.81–3.31), 14 RCTs, 2518 people — no confirmed difference, low precision. "BA drives people away" is not a fact, and "BA loses nobody" is not one either.
  • Long term. The digital BA effect fades by 12 months (p=0.82), and Cochrane gives no confidence about distant outcomes. Promising "it will cure you" is not allowed.

Not found

  • NICE NG222 — 403 on every attempt (page and PDF). NICE guidance is not quoted, to avoid retelling it from memory.
  • The full Cochrane CD013305 text — 403; all numbers come from the abstract.
  • RCTs varying the size of a step or the number of actions per week — none.
  • BA in pronounced apathy or anhedonia — no separate studies.
  • BA in active suicidality — such participants are excluded from RCTs.
  • Studies of harm from a "daily diary" and guilt — not found; Jacobson 1996 and Kanter 2010 were not opened.
